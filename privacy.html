Privacy

Sunscore: Sunrise & Sunset · last updated 10 November 2026

Sunscore has no accounts, no adverts and no tracking. This page says what leaves your phone and why.

Your location

Your location is used on your phone to work out the forecast. To fetch the weather, the coordinates are sent to our forecast provider, Open-Meteo, and to BigDataCloud and OpenStreetMap services to find place names and nearby viewpoints. They are not stored by us and are not linked to you.

Ratings

When you rate a sky, we receive the rating, the forecast it was scored against, the time, any note you add and an area rounded to about 1 km. No name or account is attached. You can turn this off in More.

Photos

Sharing a photo is optional. If you share one, we store the photo (resized, with its hidden camera data removed), your rating, the time and a location rounded to about 1 km, together with a random ID for your phone so you can delete your photos later. Photos appear publicly on the Community map once they have been checked. You can delete any of your photos in More, under Your photos.

Where it is kept

The app is hosted by Netlify, which keeps standard server logs. Photos and their details are stored with Google Firebase in the European Union. Settings, saved spots and your rating history stay on your phone.

Fonts, map pictures and map code are loaded from Google Fonts, OpenStreetMap and cdnjs. As with any website, those services see your phone's internet address when it asks for them.

Children

Sunscore is not aimed at children under 13.

Your choices

To have anything you shared removed, or to ask a question, email hello@sunscore.day. Clearing the app's data on your phone removes everything stored there.
