    How it works

    The best sunsets happen when the sun, already at or below your horizon, shines underneath clouds above you and lights them from below.

    So the app checks the forecast in two places: overhead (is there cloud to light up?) and 75–250 km towards the sun (is the sky clear enough for the light to get through?).

    • Cloud canvas: mid and high cloud overhead, ideally 30–70% cover.
    • Clear horizon: little low cloud along the light path.
    • No low cloud / not overcast: a thick deck overhead hides everything.
    • Dry and clear air: rain and haze wash the colours out.

    The six factors are multiplied together, so one bad factor pulls the whole score down. A clear sky scores about 25: a pleasant glow, but nothing to light up.

    Forecasts can be wrong, especially about cloud, so check the confidence and look again nearer the time. Your ratings help tune the model.
